About Stat Forge

A small browser tool for turning 5e monster numbers into working D&D Beyond and AboveVTT clickable dice.

How it started Why a website What it does Privacy Open source Credits

How it started

Stat Forge started after I heard my DM complaining that most of his session prep went into writing monster statblocks instead of lore or story, and that he was typing out D&D Beyond's [rollable] notation by hand.

I had never DM'd before and had zero idea that was how homebrew monsters worked on D&D Beyond. I built a small Windows .exe for him and kept adjusting it based on his critiques until his monsters pasted cleanly and rolled on the first try.

Why it became a website

He said the app was gamechanging for his prep, except there was no auto-updater. Whenever he found a bug or asked for a feature, I had to make the change, rebuild the .exe, and send him a new copy.

Then on the D&D Beyond subreddits I saw other people having the same trouble writing rollable notations by hand, so I figured why not just make it a website. Now my DM gets fixes by refreshing a tab, and anyone else can open it next to D&D Beyond without downloading anything.

What it does (and what it skips)

Stat Forge skips AC, Hit Points, ability scores, and CR calculators because D&D Beyond already has form fields for those. It only handles the text boxes in the homebrew monster form.

  • Separate tabs for Traits, Actions, Bonus Actions, Reactions, Legendary Actions, and Lair Actions, with a tracker that shows which boxes changed since you last copied them.
  • Both 2024 wording (Melee Attack Roll:, [spells]) and 2014 wording (Melee Weapon Attack: or Melee Spell Attack:, [spell]).
  • Hover over or Tab to any underlined roll in the preview to see the exact dice, roll type, damage type, average, and range D&D Beyond will read.
  • Automatic saving in your browser, undo and redo, local recovery checkpoints, a reusable ability library, a pasted-action importer, and Check DDB for repairing broken markup you already wrote elsewhere.

Privacy and your data

Nothing leaves your browser. Stat Forge has no accounts, no database, no analytics, no cookies, and no ads, and it makes zero network requests after the page loads.

Your monsters, folders, saved abilities, and recovery checkpoints stay in your browser's localStorage. Clearing site data in your browser deletes them, so use Export whenever you want a .json backup or want to move your library to another device.

Open source and feedback

Stat Forge is written in plain JavaScript with no build step and released under the MIT license on GitHub.

If a 5e wording edge case or D&D Beyond rollable tag breaks when you paste it, open an issue on GitHub with the statblock text so I can reproduce it. Also, don't be afraid to request features!
